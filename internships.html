Application Process

1. Submit Application

Complete our online application with CV, academic transcripts, and a 500-word statement of purpose explaining your interest in mental health and professional goals.

2. Interview Process

Virtual/face-to-face interview with our team. We'll assess your technical knowledge, communication skills, and motivation for pursuing mental health work.

3. Skills Assessment

Practical assessment based on your chosen specialization. May include case study analysis, role-play scenarios, or research proposal presentation.

4. Program Orientation

Successful candidates receive personalized orientation, mentor assignment, and 2-week onboarding process with gradual skill development.

Who Can Apply

Students & Recent graduates

  • Psychology students (Bachelor's/Master's)
  • Social Work students
  • Public Health students
  • Counseling students

Requirement

  • Currently enrolled or recent graduate
  • Strong academic record (minimum 60%)
  • Technical skills in basic mental health concepts
  • Genuine interest in mental health career

Preferred

  • Past coursework in psychology/mental health
  • Volunteer or community service experience
  • Strong communication skills
  • Cultural sensitivity and empathy
