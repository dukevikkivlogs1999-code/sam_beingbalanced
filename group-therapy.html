Group Therapy: Where Healing Multiplies

Group therapy offers something uniquely powerful - the experience of knowing you're not alone in your struggles. At Being Balanced, our therapeutic groups create safe, structured spaces where you can share experiences, learn from others, and discover new perspectives on healing and growth.

Our approach combines evidence-based therapeutic techniques with the natural power of peer support and shared understanding. Groups are kept intentionally small (6-12 participants) to ensure individual attention and meaningful connections.

Group Therapy FAQ

Is group therapy as effective as individual therapy?

Research shows group therapy is equally effective for many mental health conditions. The unique benefits of peer support and shared learning often complement individual therapy beautifully.

How confidential are group sessions?

Confidentiality is strictly maintained. All participants sign confidentiality agreements, and facilitators ensure psychological safety for comfortable sharing.

What's the recommended commitment?

We recommend 8-12 weeks minimum for sustainable benefits. Individual results vary, but consistency is key.

Can I continue individual therapy alongside group?

Absolutely! Many clients benefit from combined individual and group work. We can coordinate care for integrated support.
